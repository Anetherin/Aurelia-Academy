## 1. Elite Patron Buyouts & Graduate Placement
- **Threshold Offers:** Wealthy syndicate patrons and high-society houses periodically bid for exclusive placement or buyouts once an enrolled student meets clear milestones (e.g., Rank `A`/`S`/`SS`, high primary role/service skills, Depravity/Refinement thresholds, or Academic Prestige standing).
- **Negotiation & Choice:** Directors can review client offers (lump-sum debt relief / liquid funds, reputation shift, and client profile), accept the placement, hold out for higher tiers, or retain key roster stars.
- **Alumni Ledger:** Placed students transition out of active Dorm capacity into an Alumni archive (while remaining savable to the local Student Library).

## 2. Lineage Wing — Breeding Chamber & Incubator
- **New Facilities:** Add an unlockable **Breeding Chamber** (paired with patron/Director lineage pairings) and an **Accelerated-Maturation Incubator** wing in `data/academy.json` & `Facilities`.
- **Genetics & Stat Inheritance:** Mothers pass on physical traits (`hairColor`, `eyeColor`, `skinTone`, `build`, `bust`, `feature`), selected personality traits, and inherited/boosted innate Aptitudes (`intelligence`, `physicality`, `social`, `libido`) plus starting skill affinities to offspring, scaled by Breeding Chamber & Incubator tier.
- **Adult-Only Maturation Rule:** Offspring develop strictly inside automated accelerated-maturation incubator pods and emerge exclusively as fully mature adults (18+) ready for enrollment or roster assignment—never depicting or generating any pre-adult stage.
